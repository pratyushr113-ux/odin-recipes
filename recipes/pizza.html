Recipe Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (or bread flour), ¾ cup lukewarm water
  • 1 tsp active dry yeast or instant yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1½ tsp salt, and 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ cup tomato sauce or passata, 1 clove minced garlic, 1 tsp dried oregano or Italian herbs, salt, and pepper.
  • 1 to 1½ cups grated mozzarella cheese, grated parmesan, and your choice of vegetables (sliced onions, capsicum/bell peppers, mushrooms) or meats (pepperoni, sausage).

Steps

  1. AMix the lukewarm water, yeast, and sugar in a small bowl. Let it sit for 5 minutes until it becomes frothy.
  2. Combine the flour and salt in a large bowl. Pour in the yeast mixture and olive oil. Knead by hand for 8–10 minutes until the dough is smooth and elastic.
  3. Place the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it with a cloth or cling film, and let it rise in a warm spot for about 30 to 60 minutes until doubled in size.
  4. 1. While the dough rises, simmer your tomato sauce, minced garlic, oregano, salt, and a pinch of sugar in a pan for 10–15 minutes until thickened, then cool.
  5. Punch down the risen dough and divide it. On an oiled or cornmeal-dusted tray, use your fingertips to stretch and flatten the dough into a 9-to-10-inch round base with slightly thicker edges.
  6. Spread an even layer of the prepared tomato sauce over the base, leaving a small border for the crust. Add a generous layer of grated mozzarella cheese, followed by your chopped vegetables or meats, and finish with a sprinkle of herbs.
  7. Preheat your oven to a high temperature (around 220°C / 450°F). Bake the pizza for 8 to 12 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling. Slice and serve hot

Pizza is ready to serve
